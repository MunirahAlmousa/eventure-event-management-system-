About Eventure

Eventure is a comprehensive event management platform designed to simplify the process of creating, managing, and attending events. Our mission is to connect people through memorable experiences.

Our Vision

For event organizers and attendees who need an easy and secure platform to manage and attend events, Eventure is a complete digital system for event management that enables users to create events, manage tickets, send notifications, and manage attendance.

Unlike manual systems or separate tools, our product is a game-changer because it provides a smooth and secure experience for managing and tracking events from start to finish. Whether you're organizing a large conference, an intimate workshop, or a community meetup, Eventure streamlines every aspect of event management.

Why Choose Eventure?

We understand the challenges of event management. That's why we've built a platform that brings together all the tools you need in one place. From seamless registration and secure payment processing to real-time attendance tracking and post-event feedback collection, Eventure handles it all.

Our platform empowers organizers with powerful analytics and reporting tools, while providing attendees with an intuitive interface for discovering and registering for events. With features like QR code check-ins, customizable event categories, and integrated notification systems, we make event management effortless.

Join thousands of event organizers and attendees who trust Eventure to deliver exceptional event experiences. Whether you're planning your first event or your hundredth, we're here to help you succeed.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I register for an event?

Browse events, click on an event you're interested in, select your ticket type, and click "Register". For paid events, you'll be directed to payment.

How do I create an event?

You need an organizer account. Register as an organizer, go to your dashboard, and click "Create Event". Fill in the event details and ticket types.

What are reward points?

Earn points by registering for events (+5 points) and submitting feedback (+10 points). View your points in Settings.

How do I use my QR code ticket?

After registering, go to "My Tickets" in your dashboard. Click "View QR" to display your ticket. Show this QR code at the event entrance for check-in.

What ticket types are available?

Events may offer Free, Standard, or VIP tickets. Each type has different prices and capacities. Select your preferred type when registering.
